The free scan

The free scan is an automated, passive, read-only check of information that's already publicly visible at the URL you submit — HTTPS/redirect setup, security headers, cookie flags, server/framework disclosure, mixed content, and commonly-exposed files. It is not a penetration test and does not attempt logins, exploits, brute force, injections, or denial-of-service testing. Because it's strictly passive, it cannot alter or damage the site being scanned.
