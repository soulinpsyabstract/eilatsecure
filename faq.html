Frequently Asked Questions

Prices and details match what's on the main page.

Is the scan really free?

Yes — free, instant, no signup, no credit card required.

Is it safe to run against my live site?

Yes. The scan is fully passive and read-only — the same as what a normal visitor's browser already does when it loads your homepage. We never attempt logins, injections, or brute force, and nothing we do can modify or break your site.

What exactly do you check?

Valid HTTPS and forced redirect from HTTP; security headers (CSP, HSTS, X-Frame-Options, etc.); cookie Secure/HttpOnly flags; server and framework version disclosure; mixed content on HTTPS pages; and accidentally exposed .env, .git, or backup files.

How long does a scan take?

Seconds — you'll see your report right on the page after submitting your URL.

What if the scan finds something critical?

We offer a full security audit from ₪2,000: a human goes through what the free scan found plus what it can't see (outdated CMS versions, weak passwords, exposed admin panels), with a clear report in Hebrew within 48 hours.

Do you store data about my website?

No — we don't store your site's content beyond the scan report shown to you. We may briefly log the submitted URL and timestamp for abuse prevention on the free scanner. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can you also fix what you find, or build me a better site?

Yes. Beyond the audit, we build business websites from ₪1,200 (live in 24 hours) and AI WhatsApp agents from ₪4,000 — see web.sipa-os.org.
